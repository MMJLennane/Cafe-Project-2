Our story

French café culture, brought to the coast.

Café de la Crème was imagined as a cozy, luxurious café that blends the art of French hospitality with the warm, relaxed atmosphere of Mount Maunganui’s beach life.

From rich coffee and buttery pastries to light brunches and beachside bites, every detail is designed to feel refined but approachable.

Don't forget to bring your sense of adventure and your favourite beach towel!

Yes your furry friends are welcome too! We have a furbaby-friendly menu and outdoor seating area for your four-legged companions.

From our carefully curated menu to our welcoming atmosphere, we invite you to slow down, enjoy the sea air, and savor the simple pleasures of a French-inspired café experience.

“A place where slow mornings meet sea air, great coffee, and a little bit of Paris.”
